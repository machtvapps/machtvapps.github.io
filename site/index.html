Mach TV apps

Mach apps for Android TV, in one F-Droid repository.

Add the repository

In an F-Droid client, add this address:

https://machtvapps.github.io/fdroid/repo?fingerprint=REPO_FINGERPRINT

Obtainium: "F-Droid Third Party Repo" source, https://machtvapps.github.io/fdroid/repo and the app ID.
